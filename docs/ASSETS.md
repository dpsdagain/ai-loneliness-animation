## 🎙 AI Voiceover Options

### Free TTS Services
1. **ElevenLabs** (Free tier) — Best quality, emotional AI voices
2. **Google Cloud TTS** — WaveNet voices, free tier available
3. **Natural Reader** — Web-based, multiple voices
4. **Murf AI** — Studio-quality AI voiceover

### Recommended Voice Settings
- **Voice type**: Deep male or neutral androgynous
- **Tone**: Calm, reflective, slightly melancholic
- **Speed**: 0.85x-0.9x (slower for dramatic effect)
- **Pitch**: Slightly lower than default
